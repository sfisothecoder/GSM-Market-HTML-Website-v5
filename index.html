BEFORE YOU GO

A few things
worth knowing.

How do I buy something?

Browse the categories, then use the “Ask about this” link on a product, or get in touch directly. This does not place an order or reserve stock. We’ll need to confirm pricing, availability and purchase arrangements.

Are these confirmed prices and product photos?

Prices are reference market prices. Product photos show the named product ranges. Example photos are labelled where an exact model or style is unspecified. Confirm the exact model, colour, size, included accessories and selling price before buying.

What about delivery, returns and payment?

Ask us to confirm delivery or collection, any costs, warranty and return terms before purchasing. Online payment is not available on this website.
